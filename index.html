BridgeAID Monthly Ownership Tracker — owners only.

Monthly ownership tracker

Purpose. This table tracks how equity changes over time. Work done without pay counts toward equity; paid work does not. For example, COO activity performed without a salary counts toward equity; and on a low-value project, engineering work done without a salary counts as well.

Preparation 1 — tasks and weights. The second menu tab lists the tasks in two main groups, operations and projects, each with a weight. The tasks and their weights must be agreed between the owners; more can be added when needed.

Preparation 2 — baseline and salaries. The equity baseline is already defined. The base salaries per role must be agreed; they are treated as industry standards and mean gross monthly salary for 160 hours.

Graph. Baseline ownership vs. current cumulative equity share.

Table. Rows are owners; columns are periods, newest first, ending with the incubator programs and the IP contribution — the two periods before the company was established.

Each month, every owner:

  • logs the tasks worked on with the hours — start typing a task name from the second tab; the average weight of the logged tasks is calculated automatically;
  • selects their role and logs the gross salary received that month — the paid hours are calculated from the role's base salary (160 hours) and multiplied by the same average task weight, so both sides use the same weights;
  • the hours that count toward equity = task hours − paid hours (both weighted);
  • that figure is compared across all owners in the column, and this is what shifts the equity ratio;
  • approves the month once the data is complete — every owner approves the whole month (everyone's cards), so the equity ratio is always based on approved, actual work, with no forecasting.

Each table is effective from its month until the next table starts. A month's cards always use the table in force for that month, so inflation adjustments or a new EUR rate apply from the chosen month onward without touching earlier periods.

A card's paid hours = salary (EUR) ÷ role market salary (EUR, from the table in force) × 160. Those hours earn no equity.
